#### Conference note

ISME20 is bringing together microbial ecologists from around the world to discuss new findings across environmental, host-associated, and biotechnological microbial ecology. Dr. Jiang is sharing his current work and exchanging ideas with colleagues working on microbial diversity, evolution, and ecosystem functions.

His poster focuses on marine Chloroflexi, especially SAR202 lineages, and explores how their ecological distributions and metabolic traits vary with depth across the global ocean. Through genome-resolved metagenomics and comparative genomic analyses, this work aims to better understand how uncultivated marine microorganisms adapt to oceanic depth gradients and contribute to biogeochemical processes.
